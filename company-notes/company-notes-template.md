## Claude research prompt

Use this prompt with Claude (paste your resume and any job description alongside it) to synthesize research into the sections above:

```
I'm researching {Company Name} ahead of an application/interview for the {Role Title} role. Help me fill out a research doc with the following sections. Be specific and cite sources where possible — no generic filler.

1. Company mission/product: In 1-2 sentences, what does {Company Name} actually build, who is it for, and what core problem does it solve?

2. Why I want to work there (personal angle): Based on my resume/background (pasted below) and what you know about this company, suggest 2-3 genuine, specific reasons someone with my background might be drawn to this company — not generic "great culture" statements. Look for overlaps between their mission/product and my past work or interests.

3. Recent news/developments: Find 3-5 recent (last 6-12 months) news items — funding rounds, product launches, leadership changes, press coverage, strategic pivots. Prioritize items useful for interview small talk (i.e., things I could naturally reference in conversation).

4. Team/role specifics: Based on the job description (pasted below) and any public info (engineering blog, team pages, Glassdoor/LinkedIn), summarize what the team structure looks like, what tech stack or tools they likely use, and what this role would actually own day-to-day.

5. Questions to ask them: Suggest 5-6 sharp, non-generic questions I could ask an interviewer, then help me narrow to the best 3. Favor questions that show I've done research and think critically about their business, not questions answerable by their website.

6. How my experience maps to their needs: Cross-reference my resume against the job description and what you've learned about the company. Identify the 3-4 strongest matches between my past experience/projects and their stated needs or likely pain points, and suggest how I might phrase these connections in an interview.

My resume is attached:
{attach resume}

Company name: {Company Name}
Role: {Role Title}
Job description (if available):
{paste job description here}

```
